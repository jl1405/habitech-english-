Skill Focus: Listening

Using Health Technology to Form Good Habits

Specific Objective

Students will be able to listen to a fitness podcast and interpret recommendations about tracking sleep patterns in pairs during a chart-completion task by the end of the lesson.

Learning Outcomes

  • Can demonstrate understanding of extended discussions about health technologies and long-term habits.
  • Can follow a simulated negotiation between a patient and a health coach about creating a fitness plan.

Stage 1 (Speaking) Coach & Client Roleplay Intro
20 Min

Review this initial audio consultation clip and answer the preparatory questions.

"Coach: Hi there! How can I assist you with your habits today?
Client: I want to feel more energetic, but I don't know where to start. I could try walking in the morning.
Coach: That sounds great! You might also try cutting down on screentime before bed. We could check in next week.
Client: Okay! I may struggle with screens, but I'll try."
Target Expression:
🗣 health suggestions
Stage 2 (Speaking) Possibility Modals Template
20 Min

Study the conversation structure. Learn how to form suggestions using modals of possibility.

Health Coach "You could modify your sleeping patterns by leaving your smartphone in another room."
Client "That's true. It might be difficult, but it could help me wake up early."
Health Coach "Yes. You may also use an old-school alarm clock to avoid morning screens."
Grammar Focus: Modals of Possibility

Used to suggest options politely and express potential outcomes without sounding commanding.

Subject + could / might / may + Base Verb
  • "You could try a walking app." (Suggestion)
  • "It might help you sleep deeper." (Possibility)
  • "We may schedule a follow-up." (Uncertain Future)
Stage 3 (Speaking) Drafting Suggestions
18 Min

Draft 3 physical or healthy activity options for a client. You MUST use a possibility modal (could, might, may) in each sentence.

Sentence Frames to practice:

• "You could try [activity] to..."

• "It might improve your sleep if you..."

• "You may experience better energy by..."

Stage 4 (Speaking) Simulated Consultation
22 Min

Choose your role. Interact with the bot to negotiate a fitness plan. Remember to use modal verbs of possibility!

💬 Health Coach
Suggest habits to client using could, might, may.
👤 Patient / Client
Respond to options and state limitations.
