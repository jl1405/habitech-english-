Skill Focus: Listening

Healthy Minds for Healthy Bodies

Specific Objective

Students will be able to listen to a discussion about mindfulness practices and interpret key mental health suggestions in pairs during a note-taking and sharing task by the end of the lesson.

Learning Outcomes

  • Can demonstrate understanding of extended discussions about health technologies and long-term habits.
  • Can identify key points in conversations about mental health practices (e.g., "You might need to practice mindfulness to reduce stress.").

💡 Key Slide Definition: Digital Detox

Digital Detox: A period of time during which a person refrains from using electronic devices such as smartphones or computers, regarded as an opportunity to reduce stress or focus on social interaction in the physical world.

Stage 2 (Reading) Finding Balance Editorial
20 Min

Read the editorial. Click on exactly 3 technology keywords (devices, screen-time, notifications) highlighted in the text.

Finding Balance in a Digital World

Teenagers lately have been using multiple electronic devices for studying and leisure. This constant exposure is affecting concentration levels. Managing our daily screen-time habits is crucial. Experts warn that constant health checks might cause anxiety.

Disconnecting from mobile notifications is a helpful habit. To maintain focus, students could try offline schedules. A balanced routine will lead to a healthier mind.
