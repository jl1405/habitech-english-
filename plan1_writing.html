Skill Focus: Listening

Using Health Technology to Form Good Habits

Specific Objective

Students will be able to listen to a fitness podcast and interpret recommendations about tracking sleep patterns in pairs during a chart-completion task by the end of the lesson.

Learning Outcomes

  • Can demonstrate understanding of extended discussions about health technologies and long-term habits.
  • Can follow a simulated negotiation between a patient and a health coach about creating a fitness plan.

Stage 1 (Writing) Model Health Journal
20 Min

Examine the model page of a student health journal on the board. Identify the key parts.

📓 My Health Action Journal
Sleep Log Diet Goals Workout Summary

Week 1 Summary:
Lately, I have been tracking my daily habits using my smartwatch app. I have been sleeping more regularly to improve concentration. My goal is to maintain a consistent routine by going to bed at 10 PM. Additionally, I need a balanced diet to keep up my workout energy.

Target Vocabulary Verbs:
💪 maintain 📍 track
Stage 2 (Writing) Integrating Core Adjectives
20 Min

Focus on these target adjectives: "balanced" and "consistent". Write two sample sentences using them.

Vocabulary Focus: Essential Adjectives

balanced: containing equal or appropriate proportions of different elements (e.g. balanced diet).

consistent: acting or done in the same way over time, especially so as to be fair or accurate.

Stage 3 (Writing) Goal Brainstorming Worksheet
18 Min

Write down 3 healthy activities you have been practicing recently using Present Perfect Continuous.

Brainstorm Starter:

Write: "I have been [verb-ing] [details] recently."
