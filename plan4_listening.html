Skill Focus: Listening

Learning to Relax

Specific Objective

Students will be able to listen to a discussion about school-life balance and interpret three relaxation strategies individually during a comprehension grid completion task by the end of the lesson.

Learning Outcomes

  • Can describe the storyline of an audio or visual narrative about balancing school and personal life (e.g., "The speaker shared how they've been balancing their schoolwork with their passion for volunteering.").
  • Can comprehend extended discussions on strategies for learning to relax and maintaining well-being (e.g., "The speaker explained that practicing relaxation techniques like deep breathing and time management is essential for mental health.").

Stage 2 (Listening)Techniques to Reduce Pressure
12 Min

Read the conversation below about pressure-reduction tips. Count the frequency of key words as you read, and review the highlighted verbs.

Student A: Hi Sofia, you look stressed. Have you been studying all night?
Student B: Yes, I feel completely overwhelmed. I need to find active ways to reduce pressure.
Student A: You should prioritize your sleep. Also, it's vital to unwind by taking physical walks or practicing deep breathing. If we schedule our relaxation, we won't burn out."

Stress-Relief Action Verbs (Identified from the text):
  • prioritize: set sleep and core tasks as primary targets.
  • unwind: release tension by engaging in relaxing activities.
  • schedule: plan downtime intervals in your weekly calendar.
  • breathe: utilize slow, deep breathing to calm the nervous system.
Stage 3 (Listening)Predicting Key Strategies
16 Min

Look at the blank listening comprehension grid. Predict which keywords might correspond to each relaxation strategy. Focus on transitional words.

💡 Listening Tip: Focus on Transitional Words

Speakers often introduce points using transitions like: "Firstly...", "Secondly...", "In addition...", "Consequences include...", or "Consequently...". Listen for these to map the main strategies!

Stage 4 (Listening)Completing the Relaxation Grid
24 Min

Read the full transcript below carefully. Identify the three relaxation strategies mentioned and map them to their corresponding benefits in the grid.

"Let's map out our relaxation strategies. Firstly, we have Deep Breathing. The main benefit is that it lowers cortisol levels and calms the heart rate instantly. Secondly, we should implement Time Boxing. By scheduling short blocks of downtime, we organize our tasks and protect our boundaries. Thirdly, taking a Physical Walk in nature triggers endorphin release, which boosts mood and clears focus. Use these three strategies in your pairs!"
