Skill Focus: Listening

Healthy Minds for Healthy Bodies

Specific Objective

Students will be able to listen to a discussion about mindfulness practices and interpret key mental health suggestions in pairs during a note-taking and sharing task by the end of the lesson.

Learning Outcomes

  • Can demonstrate understanding of extended discussions about health technologies and long-term habits.
  • Can identify key points in conversations about mental health practices (e.g., "You might need to practice mindfulness to reduce stress.").

Stage 2 (Listening) Wellness Coach Interview
20 Min

Listen to the wellness coach interview. Highlight present perfect continuous structures and modals of possibility.

"Host: Hello! Today we are discussing wellness. What habits have you been promoting recently?
Coach: Lately, I have been practicing meditation to help clients achieve physical and mental balance. Daily mindfulness might help you reduce stress. Furthermore, many students have been tracking their screentime. Doing a digital detox could improve your sleep consistency."
Target Structures Review:
  • Present Perfect Continuous: "I have been practicing meditation."
  • Modals of Possibility: "It might help you reduce stress."
