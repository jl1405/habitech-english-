Skill Focus: Listening

Using Health Technology to Form Good Habits

Specific Objective

Students will be able to listen to a fitness podcast and interpret recommendations about tracking sleep patterns in pairs during a chart-completion task by the end of the lesson.

Learning Outcomes

  • Can demonstrate understanding of extended discussions about health technologies and long-term habits.
  • Can follow a simulated negotiation between a patient and a health coach about creating a fitness plan.

"Welcome back to the Daily Fitness podcast. Lately, I have been using a fitness app to improve my workout routine, and I have been tracking my sleep patterns. Grammar Focus: Present Perfect Continuous

Used to describe actions that started in the past and are still continuing or have a present relevance.

Subject + has/have + been + verb-ing
  • "I have been using a fitness app to improve my workout routine."
  • "We have been studying how wearables analyze sleep."

Stage 4 (Listening) Sleep Tracking Task
22 Min

Read the full fitness podcast recommendations transcript below to extract the sleep metrics for Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday, and complete the table.

"Let's break down the tracked data. On Monday night, the subject slept well, recording 2 hours of Deep Sleep, 5 hours of Light Sleep, and was Awake for 10 minutes. On Tuesday night, things fluctuated, showing 1.5 hours of Deep Sleep, 4 hours of Light Sleep, and they were Awake for 30 minutes. Finally, on Wednesday night, sleep improved dramatically with 2.5 hours of Deep Sleep, 5.5 hours of Light Sleep, and only 5 minutes Awake. Make sure to complete your templates!"
