Stage 2 (Reading) Opinion Editorial Reading
20 Min

Read the editorial. Click to highlight exactly 3 transition words used in the text (e.g. However, Furthermore, On the other hand).

Do Wellness Apps Really Help Us?

Wellness applications have exploded in popularity over the last few years. Millions of teenagers track their exercise, calories, and sleep daily. However, are these tools truly beneficial for our mental and physical health? Proponents claim that wearables build discipline and establish consistency. Furthermore, they give us real-time feedback that pushes us to complete our targets.

In contrast, critics argue that excessive tracking leads to stress. Constant notifications can trigger anxiety rather than positive motivation. Consequently, users might check their metrics obsessively. While technology can outline our goals, we must build internal drive. Therefore, we should use these apps as assistants, not as strict rules.
