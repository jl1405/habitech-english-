Skill Focus: Listening

How to Balance Social and School Activities

Specific Objective

Students will be able to listen to an interview about school-life balance and interpret key suggestions for relaxation in pairs during a graphic organizer completion activity.

Learning Outcomes

  • Can describe the storyline of an audio or visual narrative about balancing school and personal life.
  • Can comprehend extended discussions on strategies for learning to relax and maintaining well-being.

Stage 2 (Speaking)Model Dialogue – PPC
20 Min

Listen and read the model dialogue. Identify 2 examples of Present Perfect Continuous. Practice reading chorally in pairs.

Student A:Hey, you look tired. What have you been doing lately?
Student B:I know! I have been trying to balance football and my exams. It's really exhausting.
Student A:I understand. You might want to set aside one free afternoon each week. That could really help.
Student B:That sounds useful. I'll give it a try!
Phrases for Giving Suggestions:
  • "You might want to..."
  • "That could really help."
  • "Have you considered...?"
