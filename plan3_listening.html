Skill Focus: Listening

How to Balance Social and School Activities

Specific Objective

Students will be able to listen to an interview about school-life balance and interpret key suggestions for relaxation in pairs during a graphic organizer completion activity.

Learning Outcomes

  • Can describe the storyline of an audio or visual narrative about balancing school and personal life.
  • Can comprehend extended discussions on strategies for learning to relax and maintaining well-being.

Stage 2 (Listening)Interview: School-Life Balance
20 Min

Read the interview transcript segment below. Click on the keywords 'prioritize' and 'stress' in the text to highlight them. Then answer the 3 questions.

"Host: Today we are talking about school and social life. How do you manage your time?
Speaker: It has been really challenging. I have been trying to prioritize my tasks, but the stress builds up. I learned that setting clear downtime helps a lot. You might schedule one afternoon a week just for yourself. That could reduce anxiety significantly."

Stage 4 (Listening)Full Interview – 4 Tips
25 Min

Read the full interview transcript below. Write 4 tips using modals of possibility. Then summarize the advice in at least 3 sentences.

"Full Interview: I have been coaching students for years. Tip one: You could set a weekly planner every Sunday. Tip two: Students might reduce stress by including one social event in their schedule. Tip three: You may want to communicate your needs to teachers early. Tip four: Taking short breaks during study sessions could boost focus. These strategies have helped many students achieve a better balance."
