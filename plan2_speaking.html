Skill Focus: Listening

Healthy Minds for Healthy Bodies

Specific Objective

Students will be able to listen to a discussion about mindfulness practices and interpret key mental health suggestions in pairs during a note-taking and sharing task by the end of the lesson.

Learning Outcomes

  • Can demonstrate understanding of extended discussions about health technologies and long-term habits.
  • Can identify key points in conversations about mental health practices (e.g., "You might need to practice mindfulness to reduce stress.").

Stage 2 (Speaking) Polite Suggestions Model
20 Min

Listen to the model conversation. Learn how to state habits and express empathy politely.

Speaker A "I hope to improve my sleep schedules, but my screens keep me awake."
Speaker B "I understand how you feel. You might want to try a digital detox before bed."
Speaker A "That sounds great! I could leave my phone outside my bedroom."
Empathy Expressions Spotlight:

Use these to show understanding of your partner's problems before offering recommendations:

  • "I understand how you feel."
  • "That sounds difficult, but you might..."
  • "I see your point."
