Skill Focus: Listening

Healthy Minds for Healthy Bodies

Specific Objective

Students will be able to listen to a discussion about mindfulness practices and interpret key mental health suggestions in pairs during a note-taking and sharing task by the end of the lesson.

Learning Outcomes

  • Can demonstrate understanding of extended discussions about health technologies and long-term habits.
  • Can identify key points in conversations about mental health practices (e.g., "You might need to practice mindfulness to reduce stress.").

Stage 1 (Writing) Immediate Health Solutions
20 Min

Review the health scenario index cards. Work in pairs to suggest 2 solutions using present perfect continuous.

😴 Sleep Deprivation
Lack of sleep, constant late-night screen scrolling.
🍕 Poor Nutrition
Eating junk foods, skipped breakfasts.
🤯 High Stress
Anxiety over tests and lack of study routine.
Stage 2 (Writing) Action Plan Layout & Transitions
20 Min

Examine the plan structure template. Write down 2 transition words you will use to connect your action steps.

📋 Action Plan Template Layout
1. Current Habits 2. Wellness Goals 3. Action Steps
"Lately, I have been sleeping late." "I hope to improve my focus." "First, I could turn off my phone. Second, I might meditate."

Workshop Guide 5-Point Rubric

3. Topic Integration Draft outlined 3 strategies relating to chosen topic.
4. Transition words usage Sentences are linked with logic transitions.
5. Punctuation & Spelling accuracy Check for clear spelling and paragraph structures.
